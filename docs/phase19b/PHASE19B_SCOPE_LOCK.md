SKG remains a non-authoritative truth-foundation/readiness source.

under SKG constraint.

execution eligibility, governance outcomes, classification outcomes, licensing
outcomes, Domain outcomes, or Aurion outcomes.

ODA, PVPL, and SIM remain support/proof/evidence-only.

They may be referenced for local/private evidence and pilot-package preparation,
but they must not gain runtime, decision, execution, audit, token, hash-chain,
publication, or governance authority.

unless the owner separately promotes it.

## Runtime Expansion Boundary
