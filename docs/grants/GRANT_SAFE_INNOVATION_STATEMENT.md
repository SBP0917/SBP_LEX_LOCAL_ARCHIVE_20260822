## Innovation Summary

SBP-LEX Dual V6 explores a structured local validation approach for controlled
decision flow. Its grant-safe originality is in how it relates and sequences
decision-control concepts, not in any disclosed implementation detail.

At a high level, the prototype organises:

- ordered intent capture
- external truth/context preparation
- procedural truth assessment
- classification, licensing, and governance sequencing
- domain admissibility review
- constrained pathway resolution
- fail-closed execution gating
- audit/replay evidence for later review

## Why This Is Different

The project is not presented as a general AI chatbot, ordinary dashboard, or
production deployment. It is a local/private validation prototype focused on
bounded execution admissibility and evidence-supported review.

The intended contribution is a controlled way to discuss whether decision-flow
claims are supported by evidence, without requiring immediate full disclosure of
source code or sensitive architecture.
