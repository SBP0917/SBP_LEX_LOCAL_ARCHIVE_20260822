# Grant Research Checklist

Status: safe research checklist. Do not record private tax distress, trust deed
content, source code, or rebuild-level technical detail here.

## Live Grant Verification

- [ ] Verify the grant/program is currently open.
- [ ] Record source URL.
- [ ] Record date checked.
- [ ] Record closing date from official source.
- [ ] Record funding range from official source.
- [ ] Record whether eligibility is confirmed, uncertain, or blocked.

## Entity / Eligibility

- [ ] Verify trust eligibility.
- [ ] Verify ABN requirement.
- [ ] Verify GST requirement.
- [ ] Verify company/Pty Ltd requirement, if any.
- [ ] Verify sole trader/trust/trustee eligibility wording.
- [ ] Verify Queensland location requirement.
- [ ] Verify industry/category requirement.

## Funding Rules

- [ ] Verify matched funding requirement.
- [ ] Verify cash vs in-kind contribution rules.
- [ ] Verify premises/workspace funding eligibility.
- [ ] Verify hardware/software/tooling eligibility.
- [ ] Verify prototype/commercialisation eligibility.
- [ ] Verify university validation/reviewer cost eligibility.
- [ ] Verify business setup/admin eligibility.

## IP / Confidentiality / Disclosure

- [ ] Verify confidentiality/public-disclosure handling.
- [ ] Verify whether attachments become public.
- [ ] Verify whether attachments are shared with assessors only.
- [ ] Verify whether student/university partner involvement is required.
- [ ] Verify whether source code is required.
- [ ] Verify whether a full repository handover is required.
- [ ] Verify whether publication rights are requested.
- [ ] Verify whether IP ownership or licence terms are affected.
- [ ] Verify whether grant body claims any rights to submitted materials.

## Attachment Review

- [ ] Identify required attachments.
- [ ] Classify each attachment using `GRANT_ATTACHMENT_APPROVAL_REGISTER.md`.
- [ ] Remove source code.
- [ ] Remove full blueprint content.
- [ ] Remove rebuild-level architecture.
- [ ] Remove SKG/DTN mechanics.
- [ ] Remove EIE implementation mechanics.
- [ ] Remove token/gate internals.
- [ ] Remove full ABN from public-facing material unless owner approves.
- [ ] Remove trust deed content unless explicitly required and owner approves.
- [ ] Apply confidentiality notice.
- [ ] Apply watermark if appropriate.

## Do-Not-Claim Check

- [ ] Do not claim GST registration is active unless confirmed.
- [ ] Do not claim Pty Ltd/company status unless confirmed.
- [ ] Do not claim grant eligibility is confirmed unless verified.
- [ ] Do not claim grant is open unless verified.
- [ ] Do not claim university validation has occurred.
- [ ] Do not claim production readiness.
- [ ] Do not claim patent/legal protection is complete.
- [ ] Do not claim certification, guarantee, or deployment readiness.
