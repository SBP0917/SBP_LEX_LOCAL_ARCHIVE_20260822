## Fit Rating Guide

- HIGH: strong apparent fit after live verification.
- MEDIUM: possible fit but eligibility or disclosure issues remain.
- LOW: weak fit or high disclosure burden.
- HOLD: do not proceed until owner or grant body resolves blocker.
- REJECT: not suitable.

## Strict Use Limits

Do not enter private trust deed content, private financial distress, source code,
full architecture, full ABN in public notes, SKG/DTN mechanics, EIE internals,
token/gate internals, or commercial package/pricing strategy.
