disclosure of sensitive owner, trust, tax, or SBP-LEX rebuild-level details. It
is not legal, tax, accounting, grant, or commercial advice.

## ABR / ATO Call Checklist

Ask or confirm:

1. GST registration status.
2. Correct entity name for grants and invoices.
3. Whether the family/discretionary trust name, trustee name, or trading name
   should appear in grant applications.
4. Business/trading name handling.
5. ANZSIC/business activity wording suitable for the project.
6. BAS/reporting obligations if GST registration is active or becomes active.
7. Whether invoices should list trust, trustee, ABN, trading name, or another
   approved entity format.

Do not record private tax debt, personal financial distress, or unnecessary
private financial information in grant materials.
