# Audit and Export Evidence

## Audit continuity

The dashboard reports whether an audit record is available and displays the
available audit reference or hash supplied by the runtime. Together with event
and replay information, this supports trace continuity and a controlled
runtime record for the local demonstration.

## Available exports

- **Export Latest Result** downloads the latest controlled runtime snapshot.
- **Export Replay Ledger** downloads the available replay ledger.
- **Export Events** downloads the available traversal event record.

## What the exports support

The exports can support:

- review of the displayed local result
- retention of local demonstration evidence
- comparison of replay ledger entries
- review of available trace events
- handoff to an authorized reviewer

## What the exports do not establish

The exports do not independently prove legal validity, production deployment,
external authority, exhaustive correctness, absence of all defects, formal
certification, or third-party custody. They are local V1 evidence generated by
the existing runtime and should be reviewed with the repository version,
verification report, and operating context.
