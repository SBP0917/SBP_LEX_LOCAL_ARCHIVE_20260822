# First Contact Validation Questions

Status: first-contact draft. Owner approval required before sending.

These questions are non-enabling and are intended only to ask whether a private
university validation discussion is possible. They do not disclose source code,
full traversal mechanics, rebuild-level architecture, blueprint content,
unredacted SKG/DTN mechanics, EIE implementation mechanics, token/gate internals,
or commercial package/pricing strategy.

## Initial Questions For University Contact

1. Can a named academic or technical reviewer assess a private local Desktop V1
   validation prototype under written confidentiality terms?
2. Can the review be limited to named reviewers only, with no student access
   unless separately approved by the owner?
3. Can the review be conducted without publication, redistribution, teaching
   use, public upload, or derivative reuse unless the owner gives written
   approval?
4. Can the university confirm that receiving review material does not create any
   licence, ownership transfer, publication right, teaching right, derivative
   work right, or commercial right?
5. Can the first technical review begin with a watermarked, hash-manifested
   evidence package rather than a full repository disclosure?
6. Can source-level and architecture-sensitive details be withheld unless a
   named reviewer specifically requires them and the owner approves?
7. Can review findings be reported privately to the owner without public release
   or publication?
8. Can any screenshot, excerpt, diagram, or summary use be withheld from
   publication unless the owner approves in writing?

## Review Outcome Requested Later

If confidentiality terms are accepted, the owner may later ask a named reviewer
to state whether selected bounded technical claims are supported, unsupported,
overbroad, ambiguous, or require narrowing.

## Not Requested At First Contact

The owner is not asking for:

- public validation
- production certification
- legal advice
- patent advice
- security certification
- publication
- student project use
- source code review before confidentiality acceptance
- deployment support
